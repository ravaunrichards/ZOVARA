Community guidelines

ZOVARA should be a place for creative expression without abuse. These baseline rules apply to posts, comments, direct messages, profiles, livestreams, and communities.

Not allowed

  • Threats, targeted harassment, hateful attacks, or encouragement of violence.
  • Scams, impersonation, deceptive payment requests, or fake giveaways.
  • Non-consensual intimate content, sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, or sexualization of minors.
  • Spam, malicious links, doxxing, unlawful content, or sharing private information without permission.
  • Content that infringes another person's rights or violates applicable law.

Reporting and enforcement

Signed-in users can report posts for review. Authorized administrators can inspect the report queue, document a resolution, and disable accounts when appropriate. For production, staff need escalation procedures, response targets, appeals, child-safety workflows, and region-specific legal review.

Safety note

Content moderation tools are not a substitute for trained human review or emergency services. Do not use ZOVARA to report an immediate danger to life.
