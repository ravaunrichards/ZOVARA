Privacy notice

Repository baseline · publish operator-approved legal wording before production launch.

ZOVARA stores only the information needed to operate its enabled services. The operator must review this notice, add the legal entity and contact details, confirm regional data-protection obligations, and publish the final version before accepting public users.

Information handled

  • Account information, including email address, username, display name, password hash, and profile preferences.
  • Content you choose to publish, uploaded media metadata, follows, likes, comments, community memberships, and messages between conversation participants.
  • Safety reports, moderation decisions, wallet ledger entries, virtual-currency gifts, and payment-provider references needed to reconcile supported transactions.
  • Technical logs produced by the deployed infrastructure. The operator must set log redaction and retention policies for the actual hosting setup.

Passwords and payments

Passwords are stored as one-way password hashes. When Stripe is configured, checkout is handled by Stripe. ZOVARA does not ask the browser to submit raw card numbers to the ZOVARA API; payment completion is accepted only from signature-verified provider webhooks. Virtual coins are not represented as cash or a deposit account.

Storage and service providers

Account data is held in the configured PostgreSQL database. Media is held in local disk storage for development, or an explicitly configured S3-compatible object store for production. Enabled payment, livestream, email, push, or other services may process the relevant information under their own terms. The operator must list every live provider and hosting region here before launch.

Retention, rights, and contact

The production operator must define a retention schedule, an account deletion and export process, a legal request procedure, and age-safety policy appropriate to the regions served. Replace this notice with reviewed legal text and a working privacy contact before enabling public registrations.
